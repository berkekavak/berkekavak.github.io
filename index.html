Approach

Prototype early. Ship on your stack. Hand it over.

A working agent is worth more than a slide deck. We get to one quickly, then make it reliable enough to run in production without us.

01 · Discover

Find the workflow

One to two weeks on your process and data. Together we pick the workflow worth automating, the success metric, and where a human stays in the loop.

02 · Prototype

Prove it on real work

A working agent on your actual documents and tools within weeks. The decision to continue is based on evidence, not a presentation.

03 · Ship

Make it reliable

Evaluation, guardrails, monitoring and cost control. Deployed on your cloud and integrated with the tools your team already uses.

04 · Hand over

Your team owns it

Documentation, training and a clean handover. The goal is a system your team runs independently.

Open source first

Readable code you own, no license lock-in, and nothing you cannot audit or rerun.

Model of your choice

Claude, GPT, Gemini or an open-weight model on your own hardware. Swappable later, not baked in.

About

Senior delivery, without the overhead.

Pappel AI is an independent Berlin studio led by Berke Kavak. Every project is delivered by a senior practitioner who has shipped ML and GenAI systems into production for six years, not handed to a junior team after the sales call.

Founder

Berke Kavak

Berke has run the AI and GenAI portfolio at BeeBI Consulting for EU and US customers since 2023, from presales through post-deployment across five concurrent clients. Before that he was an early team member at TAZI AI through 4× growth and a $4.6M round, packaging its AI solutions into repeatable offerings and shipping retention, NLP and clinical models for US and European clients.

His master's research at Boğaziçi University on biomedical entity normalization with LLMs was published at the BioCreative VIII SympTEMIST track. He holds a double major in industrial engineering and economics from Koç University.
